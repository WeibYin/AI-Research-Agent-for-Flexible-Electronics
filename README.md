# AI Research Agent for Flexible Electronics

This project aims to build an AI-assisted research workflow for materials science and flexible electronics. The agent is designed to support literature analysis, experimental planning, characterization interpretation, mechanism reasoning, and scientific writing.

## Research Background

Materials science and flexible electronics research often involves large amounts of literature, experimental records, characterization data, and manuscript revisions. Researchers need to connect information from papers, experiments, SEM/EDS/XRD/XPS/FTIR/DSC results, and reviewer comments. This process is time-consuming and can easily lead to fragmented reasoning.

## Research Scenarios

- Long-context paper reading and literature summarization
- Experimental variable design for flexible electronics and liquid metal conductors
- SEM/EDS/XRD/XPS/FTIR/DSC result interpretation
- Mechanism analysis for laser-assisted surface modification and metallization
- SCI manuscript polishing, figure caption optimization, and reviewer response drafting

## Core Logic

The workflow first collects research papers, experimental notes, characterization data, and manuscript drafts. It then decomposes the research task into several subtasks, including literature review, experimental design, data interpretation, mechanism construction, manuscript writing, and revision response generation.

## Future Plan

The next step is to integrate Xiaomi MiMo API with agent frameworks such as Cursor or OpenClaw to develop a reusable AI research assistant for materials science and flexible electronics.

## Current Progress

The current version has completed the initial project design, including research scenario definition, workflow planning, and task decomposition logic. The next stage will focus on API integration, long-context document processing, and testing with real research materials from flexible electronics and materials science projects.

## Expected Outputs

- A reusable AI research workflow for materials science
- Structured literature summaries and experimental plans
- AI-assisted interpretation of characterization results
- Drafts for SCI manuscript sections, figure captions, and reviewer responses
